SLIDE-TITLE = What Is a Food System?
NARRATION-0 = A food system covers everything from production, meaning farming, to consumption and waste. Each stage has a different main impact. Milica follows the chain.
STEP-1 = Production
STEP-2 = Processing and transport
STEP-3 = Retail and consumption
STEP-4 = Waste
NARRATION-1 = It begins on the farm.
STEP-TEXT-1 = Farming uses **fertilisers and water**: nitrogen and phosphorus from fertilisers, plus freshwater use.
NARRATION-2 = Then processing needs energy and transport creates emissions.
STEP-TEXT-2 = Processing needs **energy**. Transport and trade create **emissions**.
NARRATION-3 = Next comes retail, and our plate, where food meets our health.
STEP-TEXT-3 = Retail and markets lead to consumption, where food meets our **health**.
NARRATION-4 = And finally, waste.
STEP-TEXT-4 = Waste ends up in **landfill**. This is why one decision, for example in a school canteen, touches several stages at once.

**The air we breathe:**

- More than **22,000 premature deaths** in the Western Balkans in 2023 were caused by air pollution (EEA 2025).
- In Serbia, premature mortality due to PM2.5 (very fine particles in the air) was **188.3 per 100,000 inhabitants over 30** (EEA data via RERI).

**The heat we endure:** climate change brings more **heatwaves**, which are a health risk for workers and learners (IPCC 2023).

**The doughnut** holds this together: its social foundation includes **food and health**, while the ecological ceiling must not be overshot.
