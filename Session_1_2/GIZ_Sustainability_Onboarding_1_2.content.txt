[Content_Introduction_Template_2.md]
SLIDE-TITLE = 1992: Rio Turns the Idea into a Programme
NARRATION-0 = In 1992, the UN Conference on Environment and Development, the Earth Summit, met in Rio de Janeiro. It produced four key results.
GUIDING-QUESTION = What did the Earth Summit produce?
NARRATION-1 = First, Agenda 21.
ASPECT-1 = **Agenda 21:** an action programme with 40 chapters, for governments, business, NGOs and citizens at local, national and global level
NARRATION-2 = Second, the Rio Declaration, with two important principles.
ASPECT-2 = **Rio Declaration:** 27 principles, including the **polluter-pays principle** (the one who causes pollution pays for it) and the **precautionary principle** (act before harm is fully proven)
NARRATION-3 = Third, the climate convention.
ASPECT-3 = **UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC):** the treaty that later led to the Kyoto Protocol (1997)
NARRATION-4 = And fourth, biological diversity.
ASPECT-4 = **Convention on Biological Diversity:** protection of the variety of life on Earth
NARRATION-5 = This changed the status of the idea.
WHY-IT-MATTERS = Rio turned sustainable development from a scientific and political concept into a **global guiding principle**. Agenda 21 became the template for many local, regional and national sustainability processes.
NARRATION-6 = Which of these results did you already know?
