## Two Levels

    --{{0}}--
Milica explains to her partners that sustainability is discussed at two levels. Both need guidance on how to use regenerative and non-regenerative resources, and on how to keep ecosystem functions intact.

<!-- data-type="none" -->
| Criterion | Sustainability economics | Sustainability management |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Looks at | the economic system as a whole | individual companies |
| Topics | growth, resources, ecosystem functions | for example Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) |
| Resources | how to use regenerative and non-regenerative resources overall | how one company uses its resources |
| Ecosystem functions | absorbing waste and emissions, securing general ecosystem functions, human well-being | what one company contributes to these functions |

    --{{1}}--
Here is one example of each.

      {{1}}
- **Sustainability economics:** The conflict between SDG 8 (economic growth) and SDG 13 (climate action)
- **Sustainability management:** A company's own CSR programme

    --{{2}}--
And one common error.

      {{2}}
> [!WARNING] Common misconception
> "Sustainability is only an environmental question." It is also an economic one: the economy decides how resources are used, how much is produced and who benefits.
