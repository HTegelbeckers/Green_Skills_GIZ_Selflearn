Onboarding to Green Transformation

A self-paced course for ministry staff, school developers and VET school principals: from the origins of sustainability to planetary boundaries, social and economic perspectives, and concrete steps for greening VET.

  • 6 main topics
  • 15 subtopics
  • about 10 hours
  • English (B2)
  • interactive LiaScript with narration
  • SCORM 1.2 & 2004
1Introduction to Sustainability2Basic Concepts and Policies3Ecological Issues4Social Issues5Economic Issues6Responding to Sustainability Challenges

How to use: “Open course” starts a session in the LiaScript viewer in your browser – no login needed. Start with a main topic; it recommends which subtopic to take first.

There is no tutor: every quiz explains its answer and every reflection has an expert view to compare with. A fictional case runs through the whole course: Milica Stojanović, principal of a VET school in Serbia, prepares a green plan for her school. The SCORM packages are for upload to Moodle or another LMS.

Basic Concepts and Policies

Main topic · overview · about 20 minutes · 1 video

  • define sustainability as a normative concept and say why it is contested
  • distinguish the key models of sustainability (three pillars, doughnut, SDG "wedding cake")
  • relate these concepts and models to international policy frameworks (SDGs, Paris Agreement, European Green Deal) and Serbia's climate law

2.1

Definitions and normative interpretations of sustainability

Subtopic · about 30 minutes · 1 video

  • compare different definitions of sustainability and explain what each one stresses
  • explain why sustainability is a normative guiding principle for society and the economy
  • distinguish weak and strong sustainability and recognise both in concrete examples

2.2

Models of sustainability

Subtopic · about 30 minutes · 2 videos

  • distinguish between selected models of sustainability, for example the three-pillar model or the doughnut model
  • explain what each model allows and what it forbids – trade-offs versus hard limits
  • discuss their implications for practice, for example for a decision about a VET school programme

2.3

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Subtopic · about 30 minutes · 1 video

  • describe the purpose and structure of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  • name the goals that matter most for VET policy, school development and the occupations Serbian VET schools train for
  • give examples of how the SDGs relate to the VET sector – for example by mapping a curriculum or a greening strategy against the goals

2.4

International and national climate policies

Subtopic · about 30 minutes · 3 videos

  • outline the central elements of international and national climate policies, from the Paris Agreement to Serbian law
  • compare the three policy levels: global (Paris Agreement, SDGs), EU and region (European Green Deal, Green Agenda for the Western Balkans), and Serbia (climate law, strategy, NDC)
  • explain what "net zero" means and how it differs from "carbon neutral"

Economic Issues

  • examine economic perspectives on sustainability, including growth, limits to growth, and alternative approaches to green economies
  • distinguish the two levels at which sustainability is discussed in economics – the whole economic system and individual companies

Limits to growth

Subtopic · about 35 minutes · 2 videos

  • summarise the core idea of "limits to growth" from the 1972 Club of Rome report
  • explain why the report is a scenario study and not a precise forecast
  • name the main responses to ecological limits (efficiency, sufficiency, degrowth) and give examples
