INTRO-TEXT = """
**GreenComp** (European Commission JRC, 2022; CC BY 4.0) is a reference framework for all learners, of any age.

<!-- data-type="none" -->
| Area | Competences |
|---|---|
| **1. Embodying sustainability values** | valuing sustainability · supporting fairness · promoting nature |
| **2. Embracing complexity in sustainability** | systems thinking · critical thinking · problem framing |
| **3. Envisioning sustainable futures** | futures literacy · adaptability · exploratory thinking |
| **4. Acting for sustainability** | political agency · collective action · individual initiative |

"""

ACTIVATION-HINT = Four areas with three competences each.
ACTIVATION-FEEDBACK = 12 competences in 4 areas. The framework was published in 2022 (Bianchi, Pisiotis & Cabrera Giraldez).

[Content_Introduction_Template_2.md]
SLIDE-TITLE = You Can Be a Change Agent
NARRATION-0 = You do not need a special title to be a change agent. Milica thinks of one of her teachers, who has started a small project all on his own.
GUIDING-QUESTION = What is a change agent, and where do change agents act?
NARRATION-1 = First, the definition.
ASPECT-1 = **Change agent (change maker):** a person who consciously or unconsciously exerts influence on sustainability-relevant changes in the world (Bellina et al. 2021; Brandt 2016)
NARRATION-2 = Second, three words for influence.
ASPECT-2 = **Footprint:** the negative, passive effect. **Handprint:** the positive, active effect. **Brainprint:** education plus empowerment, the ability to think through problems and act on them
NARRATION-3 = Third, their fields.
ASPECT-3 = **Fields:** leadership and management, education, culture and arts, activism, everyday decisions, political decision-making, economic and consumer decisions, universities, and **schools and TVET institutions**
NARRATION-4 = And fourth, a practical role: the sustainability officer.
ASPECT-4 = **Sustainability officer for a TVET campus:** implements the strategy (measures, support, funding), networks stakeholders (initiatives, events, advice on applications and green-skills jobs), manages climate protection (energy and GHG balance) and develops an integrated ESD action plan
NARRATION-5 = And here are the take-home messages.
WHY-IT-MATTERS = Green empowerment and competences matter more than content alone. Be a change maker, and let colleagues and students become change makers. Network: a team is stronger than an individual.
NARRATION-6 = Which of these ideas did you already know?
