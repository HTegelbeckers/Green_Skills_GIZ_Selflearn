SLIDE-TITLE = Enough, for Everyone, Forever
NARRATION-0 = Sustainability is not only about nature. It is also about fairness. A short phrase captures both ideas of justice: Enough, for everyone, forever. Everyone is about the people of today. Forever is about the people who come after us.
IMAGE-ALT = School strike for climate in Tartu, Estonia, 15 March 2019
IMAGE-PATH = img/1280px-School_strike_for_climate_in_Tartu__Estonia__on_March_15__2019_04.jpg
IMAGE-CAPTION = School strike for climate in Tartu, Estonia, 2019 – Ehitaja – CC BY-SA 4.0 – via Wikimedia Commons
INTRO-TEXT = """
**"Enough, for everyone, forever."**

- **Intragenerational justice:** responsibility for the people alive today. All humans should be able to enjoy a decent life that at least satisfies their fundamental needs. This is the "everyone".
- **Intergenerational justice:** responsibility for future generations. This is the "forever".

**Resource use is unequal:** people in the Global North and in industrialised countries use far more resources than people in developing countries. In terms of ecological footprint, humanity lives as if it had 1.71 Earths.

**The SDGs address these gaps:** SDG 10 (reduced inequalities), SDG 5 (gender equality), SDG 16 (peace, justice and strong institutions).
"""
NARRATION-1 = One word carries a lot of weight here.
KEY-MESSAGE = "Everyone" stands for people today. "Forever" stands for future generations. "Enough" is about the level of resources.
NARRATION-2 = Check the phrase.
ACTIVATION-QUESTION = Complete the phrase: "Enough, for ____, forever." (one word)
ACTIVATION-ANSWER = everyone
ACTIVATION-HINT = It stands for intragenerational justice.
ACTIVATION-FEEDBACK = "Enough, for everyone, forever." Everyone stands for the people of today.

SLIDE-TITLE = Why Participation?
NARRATION-0 = Sustainability is normative, so it is also contested, and different groups weigh goals differently. That is why the transition relies on constant negotiation and societal learning. Without participation, decisions lose their legitimacy. Participation is also older than you might think.
IMAGE-ALT = School strike for climate in Tartu, Estonia, 15 March 2019
IMAGE-PATH = img/1280px-School_strike_for_climate_in_Tartu__Estonia__on_March_15__2019_04.jpg
IMAGE-CAPTION = School strike for climate in Tartu, Estonia, 2019 – Ehitaja – CC BY-SA 4.0
INTRO-TEXT = """
Responsibility lies with **society, science, policy makers and industries**, not with one group alone.

- **Stockholm, 1972:** NGOs took part, a first in UN history.
- **Agenda 21 (Rio, 1992):** addressed governments, business, NGOs and citizens at local, national and global level.
- **Whole-institution approach (UNEVOC):** leadership, teachers, learners and administration **jointly** develop a vision and a plan.
- **Young people:** in Tartu, Estonia, students took part in a school strike for climate. Participation is not only a question for ministries and companies.
"""
NARRATION-1 = Here is the key message.
KEY-MESSAGE = Sustainability is normative and contested. That is why it needs constant negotiation and societal learning, not one technical solution.
