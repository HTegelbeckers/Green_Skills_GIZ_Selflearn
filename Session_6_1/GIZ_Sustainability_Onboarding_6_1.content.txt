The WBGU's report *World in Transition – A Social Contract for Sustainability* (2011) argues that the transformation towards sustainability is as deep as the great transformations of earlier centuries. It cannot be achieved by technology alone. It needs a new **"social contract"** between citizens, science, business and the state. Two consequences follow:

"""

SLIDE-TITLE = Five Strategies
NARRATION-0 = Debates about transformation usually revolve around five strategies. You will recognise them in policy documents from the EU and from Serbia. They are not alternatives that exclude each other.
IMAGE-ALT = Heat pump installation on a building, photo by Jim Barton
IMAGE-PATH = img/1280px-Heat_pump_installation_-_geograph.org.uk_-_7495844.jpg
IMAGE-CAPTION = Heat pump installation – Jim Barton – CC BY-SA 2.0 – via Wikimedia Commons / geograph.org.uk
INTRO-TEXT = """
<!-- data-type="none" -->
| Strategy | In plain words |
|---|---|
| **Efficiency** | Doing more with fewer resources (weak sustainability) |
| **Sufficiency** | Using less, consuming differently (strong sustainability, degrowth) |
| **Consistency / circularity** | Closing loops so materials stay in use (circular economy) [CHECK: Juliana – "consistency" is a common third strategy in German debates; keep or drop] |
| **Decarbonisation / net zero** | Reduce emissions, balance the rest (Paris Agreement, European Green Deal) |
| **Just transition** | Make the change fair for workers and regions (EU Just Transition Mechanism; ILO) |
"""
NARRATION-1 = A real pathway usually combines several.
KEY-MESSAGE = A real pathway combines several strategies. For example, a school installs heat pumps (efficiency), buys them as a shared service (sufficiency) and keeps them repairable (circularity).
NARRATION-2 = Check one of them.
ACTIVATION-QUESTION = Which strategy means "doing more with fewer resources"? (one word)
ACTIVATION-ANSWER = efficiency
ACTIVATION-HINT = It is the first row of the table.
ACTIVATION-FEEDBACK = Efficiency. Sufficiency, by contrast, means using less and consuming differently.
