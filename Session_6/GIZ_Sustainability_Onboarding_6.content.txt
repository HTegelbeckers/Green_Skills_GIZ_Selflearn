[Content_Introduction_Template_2.md]
SLIDE-TITLE = Three Levels of Action
NARRATION-0 = Milica draws three levels on the board. The answer to the student's question starts here.
GUIDING-QUESTION = Where can action happen?
NARRATION-1 = Individuals act through everyday decisions.
ASPECT-1 = **Individuals:** everyday decisions on food, transport, energy use and travel; participation and social engagement
NARRATION-2 = Organisations act through work decisions, for example by appointing a sustainability officer on a campus.
ASPECT-2 = **Organisations:** work decisions that change how a school, campus or company runs, for example a **sustainability officer** for strategy, networking, climate protection management and an ESD action plan
NARRATION-3 = Societies act through democracy and politics.
ASPECT-3 = **Societies:** democracy and politics, such as the Paris Agreement, the EU Green Deal, the Green Agenda for the Western Balkans and Serbia's climate law
NARRATION-4 = One idea frames all of this: the great transformation. In 2011, the German Advisory Council on Global Change called for a social contract for sustainability.
ASPECT-4 = **The great transformation:** the German Advisory Council on Global Change (WBGU) called for a "social contract for sustainability" in 2011. It is not only a technical task, but a shared, political decision
NARRATION-5 = And responsibility is shared.
WHY-IT-MATTERS = Responsibility for the transformation lies with society, science, policy makers and industries. In practice, a single initiative often touches all three levels at once.
