[Concept_Explanation_Template_2.md]
SLIDE-TITLE = Two Forms of Fairness
NARRATION-0 = Milica answers the father with two ideas. In the three-pillar model, sustainability rests on ecological, social and economic pillars. In the doughnut, the inner ring is a social foundation. At the heart of the social dimension is justice, and researchers distinguish two forms.
CONCEPT-A = Intragenerational justice
CONCEPT-B = Intergenerational justice
CRITERION-1 = Word
A-1 = *intra* = within
B-1 = *inter* = between
CRITERION-2 = Fairness towards
A-2 = people living today
B-2 = future generations
CRITERION-3 = Claim
A-3 = everyone should be able to lead a decent life that at least satisfies fundamental needs
B-3 = people who come later must also be able to satisfy their needs
CRITERION-4 = Question for the school
A-4 = Who is affected by our plan today?
B-4 = What do we leave for later cohorts?
NARRATION-1 = Here is what this looks like in Milica's plan.
EXAMPLE-A = Training places that reach the students who need them most
EXAMPLE-B = Programmes that still lead to jobs in thirty years
NARRATION-2 = Be careful with one assumption.
MISCONCEPTION = "Fairness between countries is all that counts." Intragenerational justice covers everyone living today, including within one country or one town.
NARRATION-3 = Now you decide.
STATEMENT-1 = Everyone today should be able to lead a decent life.
STATEMENT-2 = Decisions today must not take away the chances of people yet to come.
STATEMENT-3 = A miner's family should not carry the cost of change alone.
STATEMENT-4 = Keeping the forest for the next generation of foresters.
CLASSIFY-HINT = "Intra" means "within": which group of people is meant here?
CLASSIFY-FEEDBACK = Statements 1 and 3 are about people living today. Statements 2 and 4 are about future generations. (Michelsen et al. 2016)
